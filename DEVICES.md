# Device catalogue

Generated by `pktforge catalog --md` from the bundled templates, `pktforge/data/census.json` (what Packet Tracer 9.0.1.0858 did with each model) and `pktforge/data/pt_help.json` (module compatibility from Packet Tracer's own help). Do not edit by hand.

Use the **model** column as `"model"` in a spec and the **ports** column in `links`. Abbreviations (`Gi0/0`, `Fa0`, `Se0/0/0`) are accepted.

## Port-name facts worth knowing

* Routers: `GigabitEthernet0/0` on 1941/2901/2911; `GigabitEthernet0/0/0` on ISR4321/ISR4331; `FastEthernet0/0` on 1841/2811/2620XM/2621XM; `FastEthernet0/0`, `1/0`, `Serial2/0`... on Router-PT.
* Switches: `FastEthernet0/1-24` + `GigabitEthernet0/1-2` on 2960-24TT/2950T-24/3560-24PS; `GigabitEthernet1/0/1-28` on 3650-24PS and IE-9320; `FastEthernet0/1`, `1/1`, `2/1`... on Switch-PT.
* End devices: `FastEthernet0` on PC-PT, Server-PT and Laptop-PT.
* A port Packet Tracer calls fibre cannot take a copper cable; pktforge rejects such a link (E009).
* `Serial0/0/0` and `Serial0/0/1` appear on a router after `"modules": ["HWIC-2T"]`.
* Packet Tracer adds a `Power Distribution Device0` to every lab it saves; that is normal.
